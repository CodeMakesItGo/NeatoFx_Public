# NeatoGolf — How to Change Your Target Color

A quick guide to connecting to your golf target and changing its color.
No app or account needed — just a phone, tablet, or computer with WiFi.

---

## Step 1 — Power on the target

Plug in the target and wait about **20 seconds** for it to start up.
The LEDs will light up when it's ready.

## Step 2 — Connect to the target's WiFi

On your phone, tablet, or computer, open your **WiFi settings** and connect to:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Network name** | `golf-1` |
| **Password** | `neato123` |

> If you have more than one target, each has its own network: `golf-1`, `golf-2`, `golf-3`, and so on. The password is the same (`neato123`) for all of them.

Your device may warn you there's "no internet" on this network — that's normal. Stay connected.

## Step 3 — Open the control page

Open any web browser (Safari, Chrome, etc.) and go to:

### `http://192.168.4.1`

The target's control page will load.

## Step 4 — Set the color mode

Find the setting labeled **Golf LEDs Hit Effect** and set it to:

### **Solid Color**

> This step matters: if it's set to Rainbow, Color Wipe, or another moving effect, the target ignores the color you pick. It must be on **Solid Color** to show one steady color.

## Step 5 — Pick your color

**The easy way — pick a ready-made color:**

Find the dropdown labeled **Target LEDs Solid Color** and choose any of:

> Red · Green · Blue · Yellow · Purple · Cyan · White · Orange · Pink

The target changes color right away.

**Want a custom color?**

Open the control labeled **Golf LEDs**, then use the color wheel to choose any
color you like. Use the **Brightness** slider to make it brighter or dimmer.

---

## That's it!

Your target will keep this color until you change it again — even after it's
unplugged and powered back on.

### Quick Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| Can't find the `golf-1` WiFi network | Wait 30 seconds after powering on, then refresh your WiFi list. |
| Page won't load at `192.168.4.1` | Make sure you're still connected to the `golf-1` WiFi, not your home WiFi. |
| Color won't change | Check that **Golf LEDs Hit Effect** is set to **Solid Color** (Step 4). |
| LEDs look dim or off | Open the **Golf LEDs** control and raise the **Brightness** slider. |
